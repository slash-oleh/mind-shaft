# Communications

- **Be concise**: skip boilerplate, fluff, long intros, polite fillers, and repetition.
- **Match the audience**: more technical in PRs, less in tickets.
- **Be direct**: state bad as bad, not "not so good".
- **Be clear**: raise problems openly, do not bury them.
- **Be precise**: give measured numbers, not estimates.
- **Rely on facts**: not feelings.
- **Keep it simple**: but not simpler than the facts allow.
- **Stay consistent**: use the same terms and standards throughout.
- **ASCII characters only**: no emojis, no typographic symbols.
